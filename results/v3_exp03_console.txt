'live' is the median over seeds. Where runaway is non-zero the seeds are a bimodal
mixture -- some settled at the offered load and some did not -- so no single number
summarises that row and it must not be compared against the closed-loop block. The
wide intervals on those rows are the honest consequence, not an estimator defect.

'*' marks a token headroom whose 95% interval excludes zero. Intervals are paired
bootstrap over seeds, 5000 resamples, seeded.

'RM wall%' bills wall clock (reserved box). 'RM gpu%' bills only seconds the GPU worked
(shared/autoscaled). Where they diverge, the conclusion depends on the billing model,
not on the cache.
