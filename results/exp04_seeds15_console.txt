orc_term vs pred_term  = what the classifier loses against perfect termination knowledge.
bel_pause vs belady    = ranking by pause alone versus by absolute next-use
                         time. Values over 100% mean `belady` is NOT an
                         upper bound in this system: it is myopic (next use
                         only, not the whole future) and the reference
                         stream is not fixed, since eviction changes when
                         work is recomputed.
pred_grd / predict     = pause-length prediction layered on top. Both go
                         sharply negative: overriding LRU with a noisy
                         ordering destroys the signal LRU already exploits,
                         namely that an old block belongs to a dead session.
At signal 0.00 termination is unlearnable by construction, so the predict
arms there are a floor, not a result.
